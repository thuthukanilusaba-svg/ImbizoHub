Add city matching, close van-hire gaps, fix web-only bugs

Van hire
- Match trips to operators by pickup city. Operators choose a base city at
  registration and can change it from their profile when they move.
- Drivers can now confirm trip completion. Previously only the customer had
  a route to that screen, so the confirmation could never complete and
  ratings stayed locked for both sides. Added a "Your trips" section, a
  Confirm trip pill in the chat, and routing for the trip_half_confirmed
  notification, which had no handler.
- The passenger now rates instead of confirming once the driver has, with a
  fallback so a driver expecting a poor review cannot withhold confirmation
  to suppress it.
- Quotes screen no longer goes blank after accepting a quote. It filtered on
  status='open', but accepting is what flips a request to 'filled', so the
  operator's phone number was reachable exactly once.
- Merged operating_area into base_city. The free-text field let an operator
  advertise coverage the pickup-city filter would never give them.
- Operator registration now requires a phone number. Three of four operators
  had none, so the paid contact reveal showed "Not provided".

Web-only fixes
- Photo pickers did nothing on the website. Alert.alert with three buttons is
  inert in react-native-web, which maps it to window.confirm. Affected the
  profile avatar and the delivery dispatch photo; post.tsx was unaffected as
  it uses separate buttons.
- Scrolling on dealer, listing, the browse-wanted response modal and both
  admin review screens. A ScrollView needs its own height constraint in the
  browser; contentContainerStyle alone leaves it unbounded.
- Bottom-sheet modals across six screens no longer overflow a wide window.
- Category chips wrap instead of being clipped.

Marketplace
- Refuse to unlock a sold listing, including by direct URL. Hiding the button
  is not the same as refusing the transaction, and that screen is reachable
  by link.
- Chat suppresses the unlock prompt once an item is marked sold.
- Check a wanted post is still open before opening the response form, rather
  than failing after the message and photo are already written.
- Replaced repeating review rows with a ratings distribution that stays the
  same height at five ratings or five thousand.
- Guard against the same chat message being sent twice.

Branding
- Real logo in the app header, replacing the placeholder letter.
- Avatar shows the uploaded photo and links to the profile. The upload flow
  already existed; nothing pointed at it.

Site and documentation
- Published the data retention policy at /data-retention.
- Hourly health check with email alerting, covering stuck payments, overdue
  deletions, undelivered contact messages and scheduled jobs that stop.
- Technical overview, revised privacy policy draft, monitoring plan.
