Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ImbizoHub, operated by Thusis Systems ("we", "our", "the app"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to operate and improve ImbizoHub:

  • Account information: your name, phone number, and email address when you register.
  • Identity documents: if you apply to become a Verified Seller or a Transport Operator, we collect the national ID document you submit, and an operator's vehicle details and licence plate. These are used only to review that application, are visible only to our reviewers, and are not shown to other users — other users see only whether a badge was granted. We delete an ID document immediately once it has been reviewed, whether it was approved or rejected — we keep the decision, not the photograph. A document nobody reviews is deleted after 60 days. See our data retention policy for the full list.
  • Location and city: the city you select for a listing, a Wanted post or a trip, and your approximate location where needed to connect you with nearby services.
  • What you post and send: your listings, Wanted posts, trip requests, quotes, chat messages, and any photos you attach to them. Photos sent in chat are stored privately and are readable only by you and the person you sent them to.
  • Ratings and reviews: the star ratings and written reviews you give and receive after a completed deal or trip.
  • Transaction information: details of fees processed through the app, including amounts and timestamps. Payments are handled through EcoCash and Paynow — we do not store your mobile money PIN or full payment credentials.
  • Device and usage data: device type, operating system, app usage patterns, and crash reports, used to improve app stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process fees and calculate applicable commissions
  • To connect you with relevant services, listings, or other users
  • To review verification applications and issue or refuse a badge
  • To communicate with you about your account, transactions, or updates to the app
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse
  • To improve and develop new features

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Payment processors (EcoCash, Paynow) to complete transactions
  • Service providers who help us operate the app (hosting, database, email delivery, crash reporting)
  • Other users, where necessary to complete a transaction — for example contact details shared between a buyer and seller once a deal is arranged, or between a customer and a transport operator once a quote is accepted
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law

We do not share your identity documents with other users under any circumstances.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse — including access controls that restrict each person's data to that person and, where a transaction requires it, the other party to it. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below. You can also delete your account directly in the app: open Profile and choose Delete my account. This is a real deletion, not a deactivation.

6. Children's Privacy

ImbizoHub is not intended for use by children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Test and Beta Use

During closed testing, features and data-handling practices may change as we refine the app. Test transactions may not reflect real payment activity.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at:

Email: support@imbizohub.com
Website: imbizohub.com
