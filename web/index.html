Four ways to use ImbizoHub

Most marketplaces make you hunt through listings. ImbizoHub works in both directions — you can search, or you can let people come to you.

🔍

Looking for something specific?

Post what you need and sellers respond with a price. No hunting through listings that were sold last month.

🛍️

See what people want

Browse open requests from real buyers and reply with your price. Responding is free — you only pay if they choose you.

🚐

Hire transport

Moving boxes, one big item, or a group of people? Post the trip and registered van operators bid for it. Compare prices, pick one. Operators keep 100% of what they quote. See how transport works →

💬

Already selling on WhatsApp?

Paste your whole catalogue in one go and import it. No re-typing every item you've already listed elsewhere.

Meet & Pay — inspect it before money changes hands

You're not sending money to a stranger and hoping. You meet, you check the item, and only then do you confirm.

You pay the seller directly. ImbizoHub never holds your money and never takes payment for the item — that's between you and the person you're buying from, in whatever way you both agree. The only thing you ever pay us is the app fee below.

1

Chat first, free

Message the seller as much as you like. No message limits and nothing to pay while you're still deciding.

2

Agree and meet

When you're both ready, arrange the deal in the app and meet up to inspect the item in person.

3

Confirm with a PIN

Happy with it? Pay the seller however you've agreed, and they generate a handover PIN to record that the deal is done. Then you both rate each other.

What it costs

Buying on a listing is free — permanently. Browsing, posting, chatting and arranging a deal cost you nothing. Where we do charge, the fee is capped.

Staying safe

Trust is the hard part of any marketplace. Here's what's actually in place.

🪪

Verified sellers

Sellers can submit a national ID for review and carry a verified badge that buyers can see before making contact.

🔐

PIN-confirmed handover

A sale is only confirmed once you've met and inspected the item and a handover PIN is generated. Nothing is confirmed remotely.

⭐

Ratings both ways

Buyers rate sellers and sellers rate buyers, so a track record follows people across deals.

🚩

Report anyone

Every profile and conversation can be reported. Reports are reviewed and accounts can be suspended.
