3. Buying and Selling

Anyone with an account can post listings, browse, and message. There is no separate "Buyer" or "Seller" account type — every account can do both.

Buying on a listing is free. Posting a listing, chatting with a seller and arranging a deal through Meet & Pay cost nothing, and ImbizoHub takes no commission on the sale. You pay the seller directly for the item; that payment happens between the two of you, outside the app.

Contact details in chat. Phone numbers and email addresses are held back inside chat until a deal is arranged through the app. This is a safety measure — it keeps a record of the conversation on the platform if something later goes wrong — and not a condition of payment, because there is no fee to buy on a listing.

4. Meet & Pay

Meet & Pay is a PIN-based system that lets a buyer and seller confirm in person that a handover has taken place. ImbizoHub does not process, hold, or guarantee any payment made between a buyer and seller — all such payment happens directly between the two parties, entirely outside the app.

5. Wanted Posts

Buyers may post what they are looking for; sellers may respond with a price. Posting a Wanted post, and responding to one, are both free.

If a buyer accepts a response, a one-time commission of 5% of the agreed price — a minimum of $1.50 and never more than $15 — is payable by the buyer, and unlocks a private chat with that seller. The seller keeps 100% of their quoted price. This commission is non-refundable.

6. Transport

Transport Operators are independent contractors, not ImbizoHub employees, and are solely responsible for holding any licences, insurance and permits required by law for the service they provide — whether they are carrying goods, passengers, or both. A one-time registration fee applies to become a Transport Operator, valid for 12 months.

When a customer accepts a quote, they pay a one-time commitment fee of 7% of the quoted price, capped at $15 — this is ImbizoHub's entire commission on the transaction. The operator keeps 100% of their quoted price, which the customer pays them directly, in cash or another method they agree between themselves, entirely outside the app.

ImbizoHub does not provide transport services and is not responsible for the safety, conduct, or condition of any vehicle or driver. Customers use transport arranged through the app entirely at their own risk. We strongly encourage customers to take common-sense safety precautions — such as sharing trip details with someone they trust — when meeting any operator in person.
